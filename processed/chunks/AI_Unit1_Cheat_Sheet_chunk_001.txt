AI UNIT 1 - EXAM CHEAT SHEET
1. Introduction to AI
AI = machines that think, learn, and make decisions like humans.
History: 1956 Dartmouth Conference, Expert Systems (1980s), ML (2000s), Deep Learning (today).
Applications: Healthcare, Finance, Education, Gaming, NLP, Robotics.
2. Propositional Logic
Uses symbols P, Q, R with operators AND, OR, NOT, IMPLIES.
Example: P → Q (If P then Q).
3. First Order Predicate Logic (FOPL)
Uses predicates, variables, and quantifiers.
Example: ∀x Human(x) → Mortal(x).
4. Clausal Form Steps
1. Remove implication 2. Move NOT inward 3. Standardize variables 4. Convert to CNF 5. Remove
universal quantifiers.
5. Inference Rules
Modus Ponens: If P → Q and P is true, then Q.
Modus Tollens: If P → Q and Q is false, then P is false.
6. Resolution & Unification
Resolution: Prove by contradiction using clauses.
Unification: Make two expressions identical. Example: {x=Shyam, y=Ram}.
7. Knowledge Representation
Network: Graph with nodes and links.
Frames: Objects with attributes (slots).
Scripts: Sequence of events (e.g., restaurant process).
